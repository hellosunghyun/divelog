## Styling Used

### Quiet Depth Tokens Applied
- Background: `bg-surface`, `bg-surface-secondary`
- Border: `border-border`, `border-border-subtle`
- Text: `text-text-primary`, `text-text-secondary`, `text-text-tertiary`
- Accent: `text-ocean-blue`, `bg-ocean-blue`
- Stage tones: `var(--color-prelude-bg)`, `var(--color-bridge-bg)`, etc.

### Form Elements
- Select: `rounded-lg border border-border focus:ring-2 focus:ring-ocean-blue`
- Checkbox: Custom styling with `data-[state=checked]:bg-ocean-blue`
- Button: `rounded-full` with proper padding
