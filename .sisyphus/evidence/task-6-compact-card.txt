## Stage 톤 액센트 바 매핑

- bridge → bg-reef-cyan
- challenge → bg-deep-ocean
- epilogue → bg-border
- null/unassigned → bg-border

## Format 뱃지 스타일

- article: `bg-surface-secondary text-secondary` → "글"
